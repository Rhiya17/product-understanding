How ShowMe Works Behind the Scenes

Imagine you have a super-smart Robot Butler who knows everything about your stroller, car seat, or gadgets. It NEVER makes things up, answers in 1 second, and shows you words, highlighted photos, or short videos.

1

The Golden Rule: Pick the Simplest Answer Format

Nobody wants to watch a 10-minute video just to find out if a stroller has a cup holder! ShowMe uses a Ladder of Simplicity:

📝
Format 1 • Fastest

Concise Text

For simple numbers, weights, rules, and dimensions.

Q: "How much does it weigh?"
A: "13.8 lbs (6.2 kg)."
🖼️
Format 2 • Visual

Photo with Callout

For locating a hidden button, port, or checking appearance.

Q: "Where is the serial number?"
A: Photo with a glowing red box on the rear axle.
🎥
Format 3 • Motion

Demonstration Video

For physical motion, folding, assembly, or multi-step tasks.

Q: "How do I fold this with 1 hand?"
A: Exact 4-sec clip showing thumb slide & handle squeeze.
2

The Two Halves: The Kitchen (Factory) & The Waiter (Server)

Why does ShowMe never give wrong answers or freeze up? Because it separates making the food from serving the customer!

🏭

1. The Product Factory (Offline)

Working in advance, taking all the time it needs

  • ✓ Reads Manuals & Specs: Extracts every sentence, page, part, and number into a database.
  • ✓ Audits Real Videos: Checks promo videos frame-by-frame. If a promo skips a latch, it marks it incomplete!
  • ✓ Human Inspector Check: Real human experts sign off on critical safety claims and folding instructions.
  • ✓ Builds the Pantry: Stores 100% verified facts, images, and videos into the Asset Library.
⚡

2. The Answer Server (Online)

Lightning fast (under 1-2 seconds) for the user

  • ✓ Understands Question: Figures out exactly what you want and which stroller model you own.
  • ✓ Retrieves Prepared Assets: Grabs the pre-verified photo or trimmed video clip from the library.
  • ✓ Strict Delivery Bouncer: Double-checks that the SKU matches 100% before displaying anything on screen.
  • ✓ Never Hallucinates: If a video isn't ready yet, it gives you clean step cards immediately and makes the video in the background! (MVP: the finished video is shown right away with an "AI-generated, not yet verified" label while checks run afterward — see LLD §1.3.)

The 4 Safety Traffic Lights (Consequence Tiers)

Not all questions have the same danger. If you get the color wrong, nobody gets hurt ($C_0$). If you install a car seat wrong, a baby could get hurt ($C_3$). ShowMe uses deterministic safety floors:

Tier C0 ℹ️

Informational

Product color, weight, box contents, warranty length.

🤖 Auto-verified by AI parsers
Tier C1 🚲

Reversible Task

Everyday folding, reclining seat, cup holder clip-on.

👥 Instructions need human sign-off in P0
Tier C2 ⚠️

Purchase & Care

Washing fabrics, wheel removal, third-party adapters.

✍️ Mandatory Human Review before publish
Tier C3 🚨

Safety Critical

Car seat installation, 5-point harness strap routing.

🛡️ Dual Human Sign-off + Mandatory Warning Banners

Lane A

Crops & callouts on real observed photos.

Illustrated Step Cards
