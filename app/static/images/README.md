# ShowMe world artwork

These decorative scenes were created with the built-in image generation tool.
They illustrate a setting; product instructions and manufacturer images still
come from ShowMe's existing evidence and media pipeline.

`driveway-world.jpg` was adapted from the user's supplied ShowMe concept image.
`listening-world.jpg` was generated to match its lighting and palette.
Both are JPEG exports of the generated originals for faster page loading.

## Prompts

Remove ALL lettering, logos, navigation, text, chat bubble, search UI, buttons, cards, thumbnail strip, feature icons and footer. Reconstruct the photographic scenery behind all removed UI. Preserve the beautiful dusk driveway scene, warm modern home at right, distant sunset city skyline, dark blurred foliage at left, woman kneeling to demonstrate a beige stroller on the right beside the open trunk of a dark SUV. Preserve the delicate glowing warm ellipse on the ground around the stroller. Compose landscape 16:9 with the woman and full stroller in the right half; the left 45 percent should be dark natural garden negative space for real HTML text. Cinematic photorealism, amber highlights, violet dusk, premium quiet mood. No text, no watermarks, no inset images, no interface elements. This is a decorative concept scene, not an instructional product photo.
