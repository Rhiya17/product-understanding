## Battery
- Up to 30 hours: noise cancellation On, Immersive Audio Off
- Up to 45 hours: noise cancellation Off, Immersive Audio Off
- Up to 23 hours: noise cancellation On and Immersive Audio On
- Full charge: up to 3 hours (charging is slower while headphones are in use)
- Quick charge: 15-minute charge gives up to 3 hours of playback (ANC on, Immersive Audio off)
- Battery type: rechargeable, replaceable lithium-ion (owner's guide: removal only by a qualified professional)

## Bluetooth
- Bluetooth version: 5.4
- Range: ~30 ft (product page) / "up to 33 feet" (support specifications article)
- Profiles: A2DP, HFP, AVRCP, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
- Codecs: aptX Adaptive via Snapdragon Sound is explicitly documented (owner's guide: "your device will automatically stream audio using the aptX Adaptive Bluetooth codec" when connected to a Snapdragon Sound-certified device). SBC/AAC support is not explicitly enumerated on the official pages checked (see collection_gaps in manifest.json).
- Google Fast Pair supported on Android

## Multipoint
- Supported: headphones connect to two devices at once. Owner's guide: playing audio on a second device switches the stream to that device; "Your headphones receive phone calls from both devices, regardless of which device is playing audio."
- Device management via the Bose app; reconnect previously connected devices from the app or the device's Bluetooth menu.

## Wired options
- 3.5 mm to 2.5 mm audio cable (included): 2.5 mm end into headphones, 3.5 mm into source. Headphones must be powered on to use the aux cable; phone-call and media-playback controls are disabled over aux and must be controlled on the source device.
- USB-C audio (new to 2nd gen): supports lossless digital audio over the included USB-C cable at 16-bit, 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz from compatible source files. Headphones charge while connected and powered on; USB audio works even with a depleted battery when connected to power.

## Controls
- Bluetooth/Power button: bottom of right earcup. Press to power on/wake; press and hold for pairing mode.
- Multi-function button: back of right earcup. Press = play/pause (and call answer/end); double-press = skip forward; triple-press = skip backward.
- Capacitive volume strip: back edge of right earcup. Swipe up = volume up; swipe down = volume down; long swipe = fast volume change. Press-and-hold acts as a configurable Shortcut (cycle audio settings / play Spotify / device voice control), set in the Bose app.
- On-head/off-head detection: auto-pause and auto-sleep; wake by putting headphones on or pressing the Bluetooth/Power button.
- Microphones: four front-facing microphones for phone calls (two per earcup); additional built-in mics for ANC (count not published).

## Pairing procedure (as documented in the owner's guide)
1. Press and hold the Bluetooth/Power button until you hear a tone and the status light blinks white twice; continue holding until the status light pulses blue (pairing mode).
2. On the source device, enable Bluetooth and select the headphones from the list of available products. Default name: "BOSE QC ULTRA 2 HP" (or the name set in the Bose app).
3. When connected, the status light glows solid blue for 5 seconds.
- Android: Google Fast Pair — with headphones in pairing mode next to the device, tap the pairing notification.
- Clear the paired-device list: press and hold the Bluetooth/Power button and Multi-function button simultaneously until a tone; status light blinks white twice.
- Factory reset: hold both buttons 15 s total until the status light glows solid white; pulses blue when restored.

- The Bose app (formerly Bose Music app) is used for setup, Bluetooth connection management, settings, modes (Quiet/Aware/Immersion/custom), Immersive Audio settings, Shortcut configuration, and firmware updates.
- Available for iOS and Android; Bose does not publish minimum OS versions on the support article checked.
