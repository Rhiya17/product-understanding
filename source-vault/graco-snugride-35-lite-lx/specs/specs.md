The original 4-35 lb "SnugRide 35 Lite LX" no longer has its own product page on gracobaby.com.

## Child fit / recommended use

- Rear-facing only: **4-30 lb** (1.8-13.6 kg) and **up to 32"** (81 cm)
- Child's head must be at least 1" below top of car seat
- Expiration: STOP using and discard **7 years after date of manufacture**

## Care & maintenance (summary from page)

- Seat pad & canopy: machine wash cold, gentle soap, delicate cycle, drip dry; no bleach
- Buckle: agitate in cup of warm water, no soap/detergents/lubricants; do not submerge buckle strap
- Harness: surface wash only (mild soap + damp cloth); do not immerse harness or LATCH straps
- Hardgoods: mild soap and cool water
