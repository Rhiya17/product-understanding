## MacBook Air (13-inch, M3, 2024)
- **Colors**: Silver, Starlight, Space Gray, Midnight
- **Model Identifier**: `Mac15,12`
- **Part Numbers**: MRXN3xx/A, MRXQ3xx/A, MRXT3xx/A, MRXV3xx/A, MRXP3xx/A, MRXR3xx/A, MRXU3xx/A, MRXW3xx/A, MXCR3xx/A, MXCT3xx/A, MXCU3xx/A, MXCV3xx/A
- **Newest compatible operating system** (as of retrieval): macOS Tahoe 26
- Tech Specs link on page: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118551
- User Guide link on page: https://support.apple.com/guide/macbook-air/welcome/mac

## Adjacent entries (for disambiguation)

## How Apple says to identify
- Apple menu > About This Mac; or serial number on the underside near regulatory markings (also on original packaging next to a barcode label); enter serial at Check Coverage; or match the Apple part number (e.g. MRXN3xx/A) printed on the box.
