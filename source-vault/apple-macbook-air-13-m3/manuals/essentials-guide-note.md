# MacBook Air Essentials guide — availability note

- Checked: 2026-08-20

Apple does not ship a classic printed/PDF manual for the MacBook Air. The official documentation is the **MacBook Air Essentials** web guide:

- Current guide entry point: https://support.apple.com/guide/macbook-air/welcome/mac
  (This URL always shows the guide for the *currently shipping* models — as of 2026-08-20 that is the M4 generation.)
- M3-era (2024) archived version of the key hardware page, still served by Apple:
  https://support.apple.com/guide/macbook-air/take-a-tour-of-macbook-air-apd7e765329d/2024/mac/14.3
  — captured locally as manuals/ports-guide.md.
- The guide is also distributed through Apple Books ("MacBook Air Essentials"), not as a direct PDF download.

The only Apple-published downloadable PDF specific to this product line is the Product Environmental Report:
- manuals/M3-MacBook-Air-Product-Environmental-Report.pdf
  (source: https://www.apple.com/environment/pdf/products/notebooks/M3_MacBook_Air_PER_March2024.pdf, linked from the official tech specs page; covers 13-inch and 15-inch MacBook Air with M3.)
