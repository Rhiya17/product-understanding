# MacBook Air — Take a tour (port layout and hardware features)

- Source: https://support.apple.com/guide/macbook-air/take-a-tour-of-macbook-air-apd7e765329d/2024/mac/14.3
  (MacBook Air Essentials web guide, version for the 2024 / M3-era models, macOS 14.3)
- Retrieved: 2026-08-20
- The guide covers the 13-inch and 15-inch MacBook Air (M2/M3 generation). Apple publishes this guide as a web guide (and via Apple Books); there is no downloadable classic PDF manual.

## Port layout (which side is which)

**LEFT side** (per guide diagram "The left side view of a MacBook Air with callouts to the MagSafe 3 and Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports"):
- 1x MagSafe 3 charging port (front-most position toward hinge end of left edge)
- 2x Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports (both on the left side, next to MagSafe)

**RIGHT side** (per guide diagram "The right side view of a MacBook Air with a callout to the 3.5 mm headphone jack"):
- 1x 3.5 mm headphone jack (the only port on the right side)

## Feature descriptions (guide text)

- **Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports**: Charge the computer, transfer data at Thunderbolt 3 or USB 4 speeds (up to 40 Gbit/s), charge devices, connect a display or projector. On MacBook Air with Apple M3 chip, you can use a second external display with the laptop lid closed.
- **MagSafe 3 port**: Plug in the included USB-C Power Adapter. Indicator light glows amber when charging is needed, green when fully charged. Fast charge up to 50 percent in around 30 minutes with the optional 70W USB-C Power Adapter.
- **3.5 mm headphone jack**: Stereo headphones or external speakers; supports high-impedance headphones without a separate DAC or amplifier.
- **1080p FaceTime HD camera**: green indicator light next to the camera glows when the camera is on.
- **Speakers**: the 13-inch MacBook Air has a four-speaker sound system (two tweeters, two woofers); Spatial Audio support for movies and music with Dolby Atmos.
- **Touch ID (power button)**: top-right of keyboard; press to turn on; authenticate and Apple Pay after setup.
- **Microphones**: built-in three-microphone array; enhanced voice clarity in audio and video calls on M3.
- **Force Touch trackpad**: entire surface acts as a button; Multi-Touch gestures.

## Wireless connectivity (from tech specs, M3)
- Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax); Bluetooth 5.3
- To pair Bluetooth accessories, Apple documents the process in the macOS User Guide: https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/blth1004/mac
