# Connect a Bluetooth device with your Mac

Source: Apple Support, Mac User Guide  
Canonical URL: https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/connect-a-wireless-accessory-blth1004/mac  
Captured: 2026-08-28

## Connect a Bluetooth device

1. Make sure the device is turned on and discoverable (see the device's documentation for details).
2. On your Mac, choose Apple menu > System Settings, then click Bluetooth in the sidebar. (You may need to scroll down.)
3. Hold the pointer over the device in the list, then click Connect.

If asked, click Accept (or enter a series of numbers, then press Enter).

Apple also documents a Control Center route: click the Bluetooth status menu in Control Center and select the device.
