# MacBook Air (13-inch, M3, 2024) — Tech Specs

- Source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118551 (Apple Support, official)
- Retrieved: 2026-08-20
- Note: Apple's current version of this page combines 13-inch M2 and M3 details in places; M3-specific values are called out. Page states "Year introduced: 2024". Base memory shown as 16GB reflects Apple's later (2025) update to base configurations; at March 2024 launch the base was 8GB.

## Finish
Silver, Starlight, Space Gray, Midnight

## Chip — Apple M3
- 8-core CPU (4 performance + 4 efficiency cores)
- 8-core GPU or 10-core GPU (configurable)
- Hardware-accelerated ray tracing
- 16-core Neural Engine
- 100GB/s memory bandwidth
- Media Engine: hardware-accelerated H.264, HEVC, ProRes, ProRes RAW; video decode/encode engines; ProRes encode/decode engine; AV1 decode

## Display
- Liquid Retina display, 13.6-inch diagonal, LED-backlit with IPS technology
- 2560-by-1664 native resolution at 224 ppi
- 500 nits brightness
- 1 billion colors, Wide color (P3), True Tone

## Memory
- 16GB unified memory (configurable to 24GB); 8GB base configuration also listed (configurable to 16GB or 24GB)

## Storage
- 256GB SSD; configurable to 512GB, 1TB, or 2TB

## Battery and Power
- Up to 18 hours Apple TV app movie playback; up to 15 hours wireless web
- 52.6-watt-hour lithium-polymer battery
- 30W USB-C Power Adapter (included with M3 with 8-core GPU)
- 35W Dual USB-C Port Compact Power Adapter (included with M3 10-core GPU + 512GB; configurable otherwise)
- USB-C to MagSafe 3 Cable; fast-charge capable with 70W USB-C Power Adapter

## Charging and Expansion (ports)
- MagSafe 3 charging port
- 3.5 mm headphone jack
- Two Thunderbolt / USB 4 ports supporting: charging, DisplayPort, Thunderbolt 3 (up to 40Gb/s), USB 4 (up to 40Gb/s)
- (Port sides are documented in the MacBook Air guide "Take a tour" page — see manuals/ports-guide.md: MagSafe 3 and both Thunderbolt ports on the LEFT side; 3.5 mm headphone jack on the RIGHT side.)

## Display Support (M3)
- Full native resolution on built-in display plus one external display up to 6K at 60Hz
- With lid closed: a second external display up to 5K at 60Hz

## Video Playback
- HEVC, H.264, AV1, ProRes; HDR with Dolby Vision, HDR10, HLG

## Audio Playback (supported formats)
- AAC, MP3, Apple Lossless, FLAC, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, Dolby Atmos

## Audio (hardware)
- Four-speaker sound system
- Spatial Audio when playing music/video with Dolby Atmos on built-in speakers
- Spatial Audio with dynamic head tracking with AirPods 3/4, AirPods Pro/Pro 2, AirPods Max
- Three-mic array with directional beamforming; Voice Isolation and Wide Spectrum mic modes
- Enhanced voice clarity in audio and video calls (M3)
- 3.5 mm headphone jack with advanced support for high-impedance headphones

## Wireless (M3)
- Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax)
- Bluetooth 5.3

## Camera
- 1080p FaceTime HD camera; advanced image signal processor with computational video

## Keyboard and Trackpad
- Backlit Magic Keyboard: 78 (U.S.) / 79 (ISO) keys, 12 full-height function keys, inverted-T arrows
- Touch ID; ambient light sensor
- Force Touch trackpad

## Size and Weight
- Height: 0.44 inch (1.13 cm)
- Width: 11.97 inches (30.41 cm)
- Depth: 8.46 inches (21.5 cm)
- Weight: 2.7 pounds (1.24 kg) (varies by configuration)

## Operating Requirements
- Line voltage 100–240V AC, 50–60Hz
- Operating temperature: 50° to 95° F (10° to 35° C)

## In the Box
- 13-inch MacBook Air; 30W USB-C Power Adapter (8-core GPU) or 35W Dual USB-C Port Compact Power Adapter (10-core GPU + 512GB); USB-C to MagSafe 3 Cable (2 m)

## Operating System
- Ships with macOS; newest compatible OS (per identify page, as of retrieval): macOS Tahoe 26
