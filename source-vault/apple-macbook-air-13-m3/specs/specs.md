# Apple MacBook Air 13-inch (M3, 2024) — Spec Summary

- Model identifier: **Mac15,12** (verified at https://support.apple.com/en-us/102869)
- Primary source: https://support.apple.com/en-us/118551 (Tech Specs, Apple Support)
- Secondary sources: https://support.apple.com/guide/macbook-air/take-a-tour-of-macbook-air-apd7e765329d/2024/mac/14.3 (port sides); https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2024/03/apple-unveils-the-new-13-and-15-inch-macbook-air-with-the-powerful-m3-chip/ (launch, pricing)
- Retrieved: 2026-08-20
- Introduced: March 4, 2024 (orders), available March 8, 2024; launch price $1,099 US

## Dimensions and weight
| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Height (closed) | 0.44 in (1.13 cm) |
| Width | 11.97 in (30.41 cm) |
| Depth | 8.46 in (21.5 cm) |
| Weight | 2.7 lb (1.24 kg), varies by configuration |

## Display
- 13.6-inch Liquid Retina (LED-backlit, IPS), 2560 x 1664 at 224 ppi, 500 nits
- 1 billion colors, Wide color (P3), True Tone

## Chip configurations (Apple M3)
- 8-core CPU (4P + 4E) with 8-core GPU, or 8-core CPU with 10-core GPU
- 16-core Neural Engine; 100GB/s memory bandwidth; hardware ray tracing; AV1 decode
- Unified memory: 8GB / 16GB / 24GB (base raised to 16GB by Apple's 2025 update to the page)
- Storage: 256GB / 512GB / 1TB / 2TB SSD

## Ports (with sides — from the official MacBook Air guide)
| Port | Count | Side |
|---|---|---|
| MagSafe 3 charging port | 1 | Left |
| Thunderbolt / USB 4 (Thunderbolt 3 40Gb/s, USB 4 40Gb/s, DisplayPort, charging) | 2 | Left |
| 3.5 mm headphone jack (high-impedance headphone support) | 1 | Right |

## Wireless
- Wi-Fi 6E (802.11ax)
- Bluetooth 5.3

## Audio
- Four-speaker sound system (two tweeters, two woofers); Spatial Audio with Dolby Atmos
- Three-mic array with directional beamforming; Voice Isolation / Wide Spectrum modes
- Playback formats: AAC, MP3, Apple Lossless, FLAC, Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, Dolby Atmos

## Camera
- 1080p FaceTime HD camera

## Battery and power
- 52.6 Wh lithium-polymer; up to 18 h Apple TV app movie playback / 15 h wireless web
- 30W USB-C or 35W Dual USB-C Port Compact adapter included (by config); 70W fast charge supported; USB-C to MagSafe 3 cable (2 m)

## External display support (M3)
- One external display up to 6K/60Hz; second external display up to 5K/60Hz with lid closed

## Colors
Silver, Starlight, Space Gray, Midnight

## Keyboard / input
- Backlit Magic Keyboard (78 US keys), Touch ID, Force Touch trackpad, ambient light sensor
