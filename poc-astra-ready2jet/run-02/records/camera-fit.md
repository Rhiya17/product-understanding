# Practical camera fit and display crops
The studio source shows the front with the visible rear wheel to image right. Run-01 Camera_Reference views the opposite side, appropriate to the video diagnostic but not to that studio photo. Camera_Photo uses +X/+Y perspective: location (2.10, 2.0475, 1.443) m; target (-0.005, 0, 0.54) m; 78 mm lens, 36 mm sensor; no depth of field. This is an artistic projection estimate, not recovered focal length. Numeric camera rotations and projected rigid landmarks are in scene-inventory.json.

Selected visible landmarks: front wheel pair centers, visible rear wheel center, main side hub and two handle corners. Compared pair spacing, fore/aft wheel offset, handle tilt and total framing. First distance was excessive; first closer fit showed too much side and insufficient floor margin. Final fit moved toward a more frontal azimuth and widened framing. Both baseline and candidate were rerendered under the final camera, identical light, exposure, scale and AgX settings. No model was rescaled, mirrored or stretched to improve alignment.

Remaining ambiguity: uncalibrated photo, partial wheel/hub occlusion, unknown exact camera height/focal length, approximate rigid geometry, one-sided cup visible in the model but not the selected photograph. The rigid landmark arrangement is a practical comparison fit, not a calibrated camera solution; near wheel spacing and handle lean still differ. Camera_Reference, Camera_RearRight, Camera_Side, Camera_Hero and Camera_Control retain baseline definitions in the saved scene. Main video diagnostic stays at the original fixed orthographic pose for all frames; it is an approximate long-distance projection, not a source camera calibration. Rear/side renders establish coherence only.

Comparison source crop: original 2000-square view-01 to (425,150,1600,1900), uniformly fit within 565×585. Both 640-square model images uniformly resize to 585-square in the three-column sheet. The controlled two-column sheet keeps model images at 640-square. Continuous video uses one crop (680,80,1580,1080) at every stage. Animation clean renders are native 1280×720; ortho scale 2.4 preserves the 1.35 m vertical span, shift_x .13 moves the product left for text. Review sheets crop that native render to (114,0,834,720), uniformly for every pose. No source or render is stretched.

The 1600-square product stills use a separate labeled photography pass: brighter neutral floor/world and broad lights, exposure 0 rather than -0.3. They are not used to claim geometry improvement. Comparison/detail lighting remains the baseline neutral studio.
